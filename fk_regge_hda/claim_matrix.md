## Recommended novelty sentence

Earlier Regge analyses established approximate Bianchi relations in the small-deficit regime and observed quadratic lifting of would-be gauge Hessian modes with deficit angle. We show how this mechanism propagates through physical/gauge Schur reduction and canonical tent-move evolution on a shape-regular Freudenthal-Kuhn refinement family: on fully stationary four-dimensional Regge solutions the middle-slice gauge sector and the old/new gauge-to-gauge Lagrangian two-form converge as O(h^4), while gauge-to-physical mixing remains O(h^2), and the limiting deformation direction carries the expected inverse-metric structure function.

## Claims that must not appear without qualification

- "Regge diffeomorphism symmetry is restored at fourth order" without specifying the gauge/deformation block and refinement limit.
- "The full canonical map is O(h^4) close to the Dirac algebra."
- "We derive full nonlinear GR from FK graphs."
- "The result is off-shell."
- "The Lorentzian canonical theorem is proved."
- "Quadratic gauge breaking is new."
