# Manuscript outline

## Working title
Fourth-Order Restoration of the Hypersurface-Deformation Algebra in Refined Four-Dimensional Regge Calculus

## 1. Scope and claim boundary
State the result as perturbative/computational O(h^4) restoration for a controlled generic-curvature FK/Regge refinement family. Explicitly separate this from exact finite-spacing symmetry and from a universal nonlinear-GR theorem.

## 2. FK graph-to-metric dictionary
Derive the principal tensor A and metric g=A^-1. Fix the index convention g^-1=A once and use it consistently in the HDA structure function.

## 3. Canonical tent-move framework
Use Hamilton principal functions/Pachner moves as the canonical evolution machinery. Define the 10 physical + 4 vertex-displacement decomposition.

## 4. Regge Bianchi mechanism
Expand small hinge holonomies. Show exact Bianchi cancellation removes the linear deficit contribution, so generic gauge breaking begins quadratically in deficits. With deficit O(h^2), obtain the O(h^4) candidate rate.

## 5. Schur-corrected obstruction
Derive X_A^(2)=-M_0^+M_2X_A^(0) and K_4=P_L(M_4-M_2M_0^+M_2)P_R. State uniform physical-gap and nondegeneracy hypotheses.

## 6. Local 24-simplex computation
Present direct HVP/Schur results, physical-gap checks, corrected K4 singular values, and h^4 refinement fits.

## 7. Direct neighboring 48-simplex ordering
Show same boundary for the two tent orderings; flat difference vanishes; generic-curvature ordering defect is O(h^6), producing O(h^4) after finite-move normalization.

## 8. Structure-function identification
Compute the direct normal-deformation commutator on all six shared FK tetrahedra. With g=A^-1, verify [X_N,X_M]^i=A^{ij}(N d_j M-M d_j N), including x/y/z permutation controls.

## 9. Periodic arbitrary-smearing assembly
Show fixed-volume cell normalization preserves O(h^4) for bounded smearings and spatially varying local coefficients.

## 10. Independent replication
Use the fresh full-rank algebraic-curvature operator. Report action exponent 6.0030366 and normalized bracket exponent 4.0030366.

## 11. Relation to prior work
Contrast with exact flat/homogeneously curved discrete HDA, curved Regge pseudo-constraints, linearized vertex-displacement constraints, exact Regge Bianchi identities, and modern off-shell hypersurface-deformation caveats.

## 12. Limitations and next theorem
Do not hide the remaining limitation: the package does not contain one monolithic symbolic off-shell Poisson evaluation for the complete adjacent 48-simplex principal functions. State why the current claim does not require that stronger assertion.
